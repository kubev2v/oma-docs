= Questions and answers about the {OMA}

[role="_abstract"]
Common questions about the {OMA}, including release cadence, language availability, and support for large VMware estates.⁠

== What is the {OMA}?

The {OMA} is a self-service tool designed to evaluate the migration readiness of your VMware workloads before you commit. By discovering your vCenter environment, an automated migration report is generated that provides the technical details that you need to plan your journey with confidence. With the {OMA}, you can assess your virtual machine workload migration path as you plan your move to {OCP-short}.

== Who can access the {OMA}, and how is it accessed?

You access the {OMA} through the {HCC}. Anyone with a {RH} account can use the tool; you do not need a {RH} subscription.

== What types of insights does the {OMA} provide?

* *Discovery* — A clear snapshot of your connected VMware clusters, including CPU and memory, disks, networks, and related inventory.
* *Warnings and risks* — Identification of potential obstacles that could affect your migration, such as specific hardware configurations or guest operating system versions. Future releases might add more remediation guidance when it is available.
* *Recommendations* — Technical guidance on how workloads can be configured in a {OCP-short} environment, plus estimates such as migration time windows and complexity views if they are displayed in your environment, based on the inventory and rules that are available in your version.

== How is the {OMA} different from the Virtualization Migration Assessment (VMA)?

VMA is a {RH} consulting engagement with workshops and architects for large or complex migration programs. The {OMA} is an automated report in the {HCC}. Teams often run the {OMA} first to establish a baseline, then use VMA or other programs to operationalize.

== How is the {OMA} different from RVTools?

RVTools exports vSphere inventory to a spreadsheet. The {OMA} consumes an RVTools export in a supported format or agent-collected inventory and applies rules and analytics to produce migration-oriented views in an automated migration report.

== What are the two ways you can create an automated migration report?

You can create an automated migration report by uploading a supported RVTools export or by deploying the assisted migration agent in your VMware vSphere environment. Your environment might offer one or both paths. See xref:modules/rvtools-and-agent-comparison-oma.adoc#rvtools-and-agent-comparison-oma[RVTools upload compared with a VMware agent] for a comparison.

== How much does the {OMA} cost?

The {OMA} is a self-service tool in typical offerings. Console availability and labels follow your {RH} entitlement or program. For navigation details, see xref:modules/how-you-access-oma.adoc#how-you-access-oma[How you use the OpenShift migration advisor in the Hybrid Cloud Console].

== What is the target audience for this solution?

The {OMA} is intended for VMware administrators, IT architects, and platform architects who are familiar with their vSphere environments and are evaluating {OCP-short} as a migration target. You must be able to generate or export the data that the service accepts, and you must understand what you are authorizing if you use an agent in your environment.

== How does this help my migration planning?

The automated migration report gives you inventory-backed data, risk signals, and sizing context before you commit to a migration design or project timeline. It helps you identify potential issues early and establish a baseline for your migration program. See xref:modules/problems-that-an-automated-migration-report-is-meant-to-help-with.adoc#problems-that-an-automated-migration-report-is-meant-to-help-with[Problems that an automated migration report is meant to help with] for more detail.

== When should we use this in our evaluation process?

During discovery and evaluation—before you finalize designs or execution—when administrators and architects want inventory-backed signals without committing to a full migration plan yet. See also xref:modules/problems-that-an-automated-migration-report-is-meant-to-help-with.adoc#problems-that-an-automated-migration-report-is-meant-to-help-with[Problems that an automated migration report is meant to help with].

[id="release-cadence-oma"]
== How often the {OMA} changes

The {OMA} is a continuously updated web service on the {HCC}. The release schedule combines major enhancements with frequent updates to the active service.

[id="language-support-oma"]
== Language availability for the product and this guide

Currently, the {OMA} user interface and documentation are available in English. Future releases might broaden regional or language coverage. Any changes will be announced through normal product communication channels.

[id="support-for-large-environments-oma"]
== Using the {OMA} in large VMware estates

You can use the {OMA} to assess VMware environments of varying sizes, from standard deployments to large enterprise estates. Upload inventory from large-scale environments to generate automated reports and plan migrations within your own policies. If you operate at an exceptionally large scale, confirm support expectations and your operational approach with your Red Hat account team or partner.
